About EggerMath GBA Emulator

EggerMath is a free browser-based Game Boy Advance (GBA) emulator powered by mGBA WebAssembly. Play GBA games instantly in Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari — no downloads, no installation. The emulator runs entirely in your browser and supports save states, fast forward, mobile touch controls, and drag-and-drop ROM uploads (.gba and .zip formats).

How to Play GBA Games Online

Step 1: Go to the GBA emulator. Step 2: Click any game from the library (Pokemon, Zelda, Mario, Metroid, Kirby, and more) or drag-and-drop your own .gba / .zip ROM file. Step 3: Play instantly with keyboard controls (WASD/Arrows for the D-Pad, K = A button, J = B button, Enter = Start) or touch controls on mobile.

Save progress with F5 (save), F9 (load), and F7 (switch between 10 slots). Progress also auto-saves every 30 seconds. Use the fast-forward button for 4x speed, or go fullscreen for the best experience.
