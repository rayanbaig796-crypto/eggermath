About EggerMath

EggerMath is a free browser gaming platform launched in 2026. We host 18000+ browser games across 14 categories, plus 8 retro console emulators (PSP, GBA, NES, SNES, N64, Nintendo DS, Sega Genesis, PlayStation 1) that run entirely in the browser via WebAssembly. No downloads, no sign-ups, no hassle.

Our mission is to make gaming accessible to everyone — whether you have five minutes or an entire afternoon. Every game loads instantly in your browser with zero friction.
