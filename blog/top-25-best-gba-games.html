Top 25 Best Game Boy Advance Games of All Time

Published August 7, 2026

The Game Boy Advance had one of the most impressive libraries in gaming history. Here are the 25 best GBA games ever made.

1. The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap

Capcom and Nintendo teamed up to create one of the best Zelda games ever. The Minish Cap features brilliant dungeon design, the unique Minish shrinking mechanic, and stunning visuals that push the GBA to its limits.

2. Metroid Fusion

A masterclass in atmosphere and tension. Metroid Fusion tells a gripping story of Samus battling the X Parasites aboard a space station. The SA-X chase sequences are genuinely terrifying.

3. Metroid Zero Mission

A ground-up remake of the original Metroid that modernizes everything while preserving the exploration that made the series great. Tighter controls, better level design, and a post-game bonus area.

4. Pokemon Emerald

The definitive Pokemon experience on GBA. Emerald combines Ruby and Sapphire with the Battle Frontier, giving hundreds of hours of post-game content. The Hoenn region has never looked better.

5. Final Fantasy Tactics Advance

A tactical RPG masterpiece. Build your clan, master the job system, and navigate a branching story with multiple endings. The Law system adds a unique strategic layer to every battle.

6. Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow

The best Castlevania on GBA. The Tactical Soul system lets you absorb enemy abilities, creating deep customization. Multiple endings and a hidden Julius mode add replay value.

7. Advance Wars 2: Black Hole Rising

Turn-based strategy at its finest. More commanding officers, bigger campaigns, and the best balance in the series. The enemy AI is challenging without being frustrating.

8. Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3

The best version of the best Mario game. Updated graphics, new power-ups, and e-Reader support with custom levels. This is how SMB3 was meant to be played.

9. Pokemon FireRed / LeafGreen

A faithful remake of the originals with modern additions. The Sevii Islands add new content, and the wireless adapter support was revolutionary for its time.

10. Mega Man Battle Network 3

The peak of the Battle Network series. Two versions with different content, the deepest chip system, and the best story in the franchise. The competitive meta is still discussed today.

11. Fire Emblem

The game that brought Fire Emblem to the West. Marq's story is compelling, the permadeath mechanic adds real stakes, and the character relationships are wonderfully written.

12. Golden Sun: The Lost Age

Bigger, better, and more epic than the first. The Djinn system creates incredible party customization, and the puzzles are some of the best on GBA.

13. WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$

A revolutionary game that redefined what a game could be. Hundreds of micro-games lasting seconds each create an addictive, hilarious experience.

14. Kirby and the Amazing Mirror

A Metroidvania Kirby game with four Kirbys navigating a massive interconnected map. Copy abilities, maze exploration, and local multiplayer make this a standout.

15. Final Fantasy IV Advance

An excellent port with bonus content, new dungeons, and the After Years epilogue. The GBA version is widely considered the best way to play this classic.

16. Kingdom Hearts: Chain of Memories

A unique card-based combat system that works surprisingly well on handheld. The story bridges Kingdom Hearts 1 and 2 perfectly.

17. Sonic Advance 2

The fastest Sonic game on handheld. Momentum-based physics, boost mechanics, and branching paths make this the definitive portable Sonic experience.

18. Phoenix Wright: Ace Attorney

Objection! The courtroom drama that started it all. Brilliant writing, memorable characters, and cases that keep you guessing until the end.

19. Tactics Ogre: The Knight of Lodis

A deep tactical RPG with a branching story and class system. One of the most underrated GBA titles.

20. Mario Kart: Super Circuit

Fast, fun, and packed with content. The retro tracks from SNES Mario Kart are a fantastic addition. Still the best portable Mario Kart.

21. Dragon Ball Z: Supersonic Warriors

The best DBZ game on GBA. Fast-paced battles, team mechanics, and story mode that covers the entire anime arc.

22. Castlevania: Harmony of Dissonance

Juste Belmont's adventure features two castles to explore, a magic system, and the best music on GBA Castlevania.

23. TMNT: Battle Nexus

A surprisingly good beat-em-up with all four turtles, combo systems, and co-op play.

24. Doom

An impressive port that proved the GBA could handle FPS games. All the classic levels with portable controls.

25. Bomberman Tournament

A unique blend of Bomberman action, RPG elements, and adventure game exploration. One of the most creative GBA titles.
