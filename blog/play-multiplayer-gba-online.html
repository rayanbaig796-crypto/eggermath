How to Play Multiplayer GBA Games Online

Published August 7, 2026

The original GBA supported multiplayer through link cables. Today, you can play those same multiplayer games with friends online.

How GBA Multiplayer Worked

The original GBA had a link cable port that allowed up to 4 GBAs to connect for multiplayer. Games like Pokemon, Mario Kart, and Mario Party used this for competitive and cooperative play.

The link cable transmitted game data between consoles in real-time. Each console ran its own copy of the game, and the link cable synchronized the game state between them.

Online Multiplayer via Emulation

Modern emulators can simulate link cable connections over the internet. Here's how it works:

  • Local link - Run multiple emulator instances on the same computer
  • Online link - Connect to a server that relays link cable data between remote players
  • Browser-based - Some browser emulators support multiplayer through WebSockets

Best Multiplayer GBA Games

Mario Kart: Super Circuit

The definitive multiplayer GBA racer. Up to 4 players can race on any track. The wireless adapter support means no tangles of link cables.

Pokemon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald

Trade Pokemon, battle friends, and participate in contests. The wireless adapter in the original games enabled cord-free multiplayer.

Super Mario Advance series

Cooperative play in Super Mario Bros. 3 and Super Mario World. One player controls Mario, the other controls Luigi simultaneously.

WarioWare Inc.

Competitive micro-game battles. The fast-paced gameplay creates hilarious multiplayer moments.

Final Fantasy Tactics Advance

Some versions support linked battles where you can fight against your friend's customized clan.

Current Limitations

Online GBA multiplayer is still experimental in most emulators. Some limitations include:

  • Not all games are supported
  • Latency can affect fast-paced games
  • Setup can be complex for non-technical users
  • Browser-based multiplayer is still in early development

The Future of GBA Multiplayer

As WebAssembly and WebRTC technologies improve, browser-based GBA multiplayer will become more accessible. Projects like EmulatorJS are actively working on netplay features that will make playing with friends as easy as sharing a link.
