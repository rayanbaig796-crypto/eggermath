Play Game Boy Advance Games on PC for Free

Published August 7, 2026

The Game Boy Advance library is vast and beloved. Here's how to play those classic games on your PC without spending a dime.

Option 1: Browser-Based (Recommended)

The easiest way to play GBA games on PC is through a browser-based emulator. Navigate to the emulator page, upload your ROM, and start playing. No installation required.

Pros: Zero setup, works instantly, always updated, sandboxed security.

Cons: Requires internet for initial load, slightly less configuration options.

Option 2: Downloaded Emulator

Downloaded emulators like mGBA or VisualBoyAdvance-M offer more control but require setup:

  • Download the emulator from the official source
  • Extract the files (usually a .zip or .7z archive)
  • Run the executable
  • Configure controls, video, and audio settings
  • Load your ROM file

Pros: Offline play, more settings, cheat code support.

Cons: Download risk, setup complexity, manual updates.

What ROM Formats Are Supported?

GBA ROMs come in two main formats:

  • .gba - The raw ROM file. Works everywhere.
  • .zip - Compressed ROM. Supported by most emulators including browser-based ones.

Controller Support

Both browser and downloaded emulators support USB and Bluetooth controllers. Connect your controller, and the emulator will detect it automatically. Popular controllers include Xbox, PlayStation, and 8BitDo controllers.

Performance

Modern PCs can run GBA emulators at full speed with no issues. Even low-end laptops from the past 5 years can handle GBA emulation perfectly. The GBA was a relatively simple system, so emulation overhead is minimal.

Legal Considerations

Emulation is legal. ROM ownership depends on your jurisdiction. If you own the original cartridge, creating a backup for personal use is generally considered fair use. Always check your local laws.
