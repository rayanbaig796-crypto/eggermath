GBA vs GBC: What Is the Difference?

Published August 7, 2026

The Game Boy Color and Game Boy Advance are both iconic Nintendo handhelds, but they represent very different eras of portable gaming. Here's what changed.

Hardware Specifications

Processor

  • GBC: Sharp LR35902 at 8 MHz (based on Z80)
  • GBA: ARM7TDMI at 16 MHz (32-bit ARM)

The GBA's processor is a full 32-bit ARM chip, compared to the GBC's 8-bit Z80-derived processor. This is a generational leap in processing power.

Memory

  • GBC: 8KB work RAM + 16KB video RAM
  • GBA: 256KB work RAM + 96KB video RAM

The GBA has over 14x the RAM of the GBC. This allows for vastly more complex games with larger worlds and more detailed graphics.

Display

  • GBC: 160x144 pixels, 56 colors on screen (from palette of 32,768)
  • GBA: 240x160 pixels, 32,768 colors on screen

The GBA's screen is larger, wider, and can display millions more colors. The GBA's display is also brighter with better contrast.

Game Library

The most obvious difference is the game library. The GBC had backward compatibility with the entire Game Boy library, plus its own exclusive titles. The GBA continued this tradition with backward compatibility with both Game Boy and Game Boy Color games.

Notable GBC Exclusives

  • The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening DX
  • Pokemon Gold/Silver/Crystal
  • Super Mario Bros. Deluxe
  • Wario Land 3
  • Donkey Kong Country

Notable GBA Exclusives

  • The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap
  • Metroid Fusion / Zero Mission
  • Pokemon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald
  • Advance Wars 1 and 2
  • Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow

Physical Design

  • GBC: Vertical orientation, small screen, D-pad and two buttons
  • GBA: Horizontal orientation, wider screen, shoulder buttons (L/R), four face buttons

The GBA introduced shoulder buttons, enabling more complex control schemes. The horizontal design was more comfortable for longer play sessions.

Battery Life

  • GBC: ~30 hours on 2 AA batteries
  • GBA: ~15 hours on 2 AA batteries (less with backlight on later models)

The GBC had significantly better battery life due to its simpler hardware and smaller screen.
