Best GBA Games for Speedrunning

Published August 7, 2026

Speedrunning on GBA is thriving. These games have the most active communities, most satisfying routes, and best entry points for new runners.

Metroid Zero Mission

The most popular GBA speedgame. Zero Mission's movement is incredibly tight, and the route has been optimized to under 1 hour for any%. The game has multiple difficulty categories and plenty of sequence breaks to discover.

  • Any% record: ~50 minutes
  • Categories: Any%, 100%, Low%, Hard Mode
  • Beginner friendly: Yes - the movement is easy to learn

Metroid Fusion

More linear than Zero Mission but with incredible tech. The SA-X sequences create exciting moments, and the movement is just as satisfying.

  • Any% record: ~1 hour
  • Categories: Any%, 100%, Hard Mode
  • Beginner friendly: Moderate

Pokemon Emerald

Pokemon speedrunning is a different beast. The RNG manipulation required for optimal runs makes this one of the most technical speedgames. The any% route involves precise inputs to manipulate encounters and critical hits.

  • Any% record: ~3.5 hours
  • Categories: Any%, 100%, Professor Oak, Nuzlocke
  • Beginner friendly: No - heavy RNG manipulation

The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap

An underappreciated speedgame. The Minish Cap has great movement tech with the rolling and the cane of Byrna, and the dungeon routing is satisfying to optimize.

  • Any% record: ~2 hours
  • Categories: Any%, 100%, All Dungeons
  • Beginner friendly: Yes

Super Mario Advance 4

Classic Mario speedrunning on handheld. The physics are slightly different from the SNES original, creating unique routing possibilities.

  • Any% record: ~2.5 hours
  • Categories: Any%, All Coins, Warpless
  • Beginner friendly: Yes - if you've run SMB3 before

Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow

The soul system creates wild speedrun strats. Getting the right souls early can completely break the game open.

  • Any% record: ~30 minutes
  • Categories: Any%, All Souls, Hard Mode
  • Beginner friendly: Moderate

Getting Started with GBA Speedrunning

  1. Pick a game you enjoy playing casually
  2. Watch existing speedruns on YouTube or Twitch
  3. Learn the basic route and key glitches
  4. Practice specific segments
  5. Join the speedrun.com community for the game
  6. Use an emulator with accurate timing for your first runs
