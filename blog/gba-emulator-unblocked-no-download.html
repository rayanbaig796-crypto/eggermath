Why Most GBA Emulators Get Blocked

School and work IT departments block GBA emulators for two reasons:

  • Downloads: Most emulators require downloading .exe or .apk files — these get flagged by antivirus and blocked by firewalls

Browser-based emulators solve both problems because nothing is downloaded and the emulator runs as JavaScript inside your browser tab.
