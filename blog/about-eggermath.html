About EggerMath

July 2026

EggerMath is a free browser gaming platform built by a small team of passionate gamers who believe that playing great games shouldn't require downloads, accounts, or payments. We launched in 2026 with a simple mission: make browser gaming accessible, discoverable, and fun for everyone.

Our Story: We noticed that finding quality browser games was surprisingly difficult. Games were scattered across dozens of websites, many filled with intrusive ads and broken links. We wanted to create a single destination where players could discover, play, and save their favorite games without any friction. That idea became EggerMath.

What We Offer: Today, EggerMath hosts over 18,000+ carefully curated games across 14 categories. Every game on our platform is sourced from trusted providers and tested to ensure it works properly. We've built features like community voting, trending charts, favorites, and powerful search to help you find exactly what you're looking for.

Our Technology: We use modern web technologies to deliver a fast, responsive experience. Games load instantly through secure iframes, our search engine processes queries in milliseconds, and the entire platform works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Under the hood, we use Supabase for our database and cloud services, ensuring your data is safe and always available.

Our Values: Privacy is paramount. We collect minimal data and use anonymous identifiers for all user features. Your favorites and votes are tied to an anonymous ID — not your personal information. We also believe in supporting game developers, which is why we partner with studios and independent creators to feature their games on our platform.

What's Next: We're constantly working to improve EggerMath. Upcoming features include user profiles, achievement systems, game recommendations, and more. We're also expanding our game library and forging new partnerships with game developers to bring you even more quality content.

Get Involved: EggerMath is built for its community. Your votes help surface the best games, your favorites inform our recommendations, and your feedback drives our development. We love hearing from players — whether it's suggestions for new features, reports of bugs, or just messages saying you found a game you love.

Thank you for being part of the EggerMath community. We're just getting started, and we can't wait to show you what's next!
