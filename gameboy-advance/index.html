The Game Boy Advance (GBA) is a 32-bit handheld console released by Nintendo in 2001 as the successor to the Game Boy Color. It features a 240x160 pixel screen capable of 32,768 colors, a 32-bit ARM7TDMI processor, and a library of over 1,500 games. The GBA dominated the handheld market, selling over 81 million units worldwide. Its most iconic games include Pokemon Ruby and Sapphire, Zelda: The Minish Cap, Metroid Fusion, and Mario Kart: Super Circuit.
