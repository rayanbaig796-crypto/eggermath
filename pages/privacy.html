Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

EggerMath ("we", "our", or "us") operates the eggermath.com website (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our browser-based Game Boy Advance emulator. Please read this policy carefully. By accessing or using the Service, you agree to the practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

Analytics Data. We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized usage information about how visitors interact with our website. This may include your IP address (which is anonymized), browser type and version, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, time spent on each page, date and time of your visit, and general geographic region. This data is collected automatically and is used solely to understand traffic patterns and improve our Service.

Local Storage (IndexedDB). The emulator stores data entirely within your browser using IndexedDB and localStorage. This includes your game save states, ROM files you upload, emulator configuration preferences (such as display scaling, key bindings, and audio settings), and your cookie consent selection. This data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Cookie Consent. We use a single functional cookie to remember your cookie consent preference so that we do not re-prompt you on subsequent visits.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Service and its features
  • To analyze website traffic and usage trends to optimize performance and user experience
  • To remember your emulator preferences and cookie consent selections
  • To detect and prevent misuse, abuse, or security threats
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, or any form of automated decision-making.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookie — A single cookie to store your cookie consent preference. This is strictly necessary for the Service to function as intended.

We do not use any form of cross-site tracking, fingerprinting, or remarketing technologies. You may disable cookies at any time through your browser settings, though doing so may affect certain site functionality.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your emulator data (save states, ROMs, preferences) is stored entirely on your device via IndexedDB. This data is never uploaded to our servers. We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect any server-side data we do hold, including SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Google Analytics data is retained for 26 months in accordance with Google's default settings, after which it is automatically deleted. Server access logs are retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging purposes and are then permanently deleted. Local storage data persists on your device until you manually clear your browser data or until the Service is updated in a way that alters storage schema.
