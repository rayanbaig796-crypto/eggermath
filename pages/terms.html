1. Description of Service

EggerMath provides a free, browser-based Game Boy Advance emulator built on the open-source mGBA engine compiled to WebAssembly. Users may upload their own ROM files from their local device to be loaded into the emulator. The emulator runs entirely client-side in your browser; no game data is transmitted to or stored on our servers.
