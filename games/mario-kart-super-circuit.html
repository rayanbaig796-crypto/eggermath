About Mario Kart Super Circuit

Mario Kart Super Circuit is the first portable Mario Kart game, featuring 20 tracks from Super Mario Kart on SNES plus 20 new courses. Drift around corners, use items to hinder opponents, and race through colorful tracks in this beloved racer. The GBA entry introduced the shortcut system and remains one of the most addictive portable racing games ever made.
