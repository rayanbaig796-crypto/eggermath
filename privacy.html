Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

EggerMath ("we", "our", "us") operates the eggermath.com website. This page explains our policies regarding data, cookies, and local storage.

What We Collect

We collect minimal data. Here's exactly what we access:

  • Analytics — We use Google Analytics to understand traffic patterns (pages visited, time spent, browser type). This data is anonymous.
  • Local Storage — Your emulator settings, save states, and preferences are stored in your browser using IndexedDB. This data never leaves your device.

What We Don't Collect

  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not require accounts or sign-ups
  • We do not track you across websites
  • We do not sell any data

Cookies

We use a single cookie to remember your cookie consent preference. Google Analytics may set its own cookies for analytics purposes.

Local Storage

Your save states, ROM files, and emulator settings are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. This data is entirely client-side and is never uploaded to our servers. If you clear your browser data, these saves will be lost.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics — Anonymous traffic analytics
  • MEGA — Some game files are loaded from MEGA cloud storage

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us.
