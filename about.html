About EggerMath

EggerMath is a free, browser-based Game Boy Advance emulator. No downloads. No installs. Just open the site and play.

What It Is

EggerMath runs the mGBA emulator engine compiled to WebAssembly, letting you play GBA games directly in your browser on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop.

Features

  • Runs in your browser — No software to install
  • Save states — Save and load your progress anytime
  • Fast forward — Speed through slow sections
  • Mobile touch controls — Play on your phone with on-screen buttons
  • Custom keyboard controls — Remap keys to your preference
  • Auto-save — Your progress is saved automatically
  • ROM upload — Drag and drop your own .gba or .zip files

How It Works

The emulator is built on mGBA, an open-source GBA emulator known for its accuracy and performance. We compile it to WebAssembly so it runs at near-native speed in any modern browser.

Your saves are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Open Source

The mGBA emulator engine is open source and licensed under the MPL 2.0. EggerMath is the web frontend that makes it easy to use in a browser.
